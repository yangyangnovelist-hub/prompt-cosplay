01 / The distinction

A good costume is not a shipping record.

The ecosystem keeps counting personas. PromptCosplay counts evidence. It refuses to turn a long prompt, a passing lint check, and a successful task into the same claim.

01

Contract

Concrete workflow, deliverables, tools, boundaries, failure handling, and acceptance gates. A role can be excellent here and still be unproven.

02

Proof

Role-specific evals, tests, or benchmarks. Generic repository CI does not prove every specialist hiding in the catalog.

03

Use

Observed invocations and errors imported from SkillReaper. What fired in real work outranks what merely looked impressive on paper.

03 / Verdicts

Every role leaves with a decision.

No vague “quality score” graveyard. Each label points to the next rational action.

SHIP

Keep the claim.

Strong contract plus a matching eval or successful observed real-world use, with no skill errors.

PROVE

Run the fight.

The role may be good. Make it beat a direct baseline before promoting it.

MERGE

End the casting call.

High overlap with a stronger role. Consolidate instead of multiplying personas.

BURY

Lose the costume.

Too little operational substance to justify its prompt and routing surface.

Make every agent earn its tokens.
